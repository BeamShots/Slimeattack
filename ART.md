# Growing Worlds artwork

`meadow.webp` is the new original home illustration. It was generated with the built-in image generation tool, inspected and encoded as WebP for the game. It is included beside index.html. The reference screenshots guided the simpler layout and cheerful mood; their branding, logos and interface graphics were not copied.

Production prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait background illustration for a friendly mobile slime adventure game's HOME screen. Create an original cheerful, simple polished casual mobile-game painting. A small round aqua slime with tiny translucent wings sits on a grassy circular clearing in the lower middle. A sunny winding sand path leads through rounded mint-green trees, soft rolling hills and a small violet stone portal in the distance; a tiny cozy mushroom workshop is on the left and a calm fishing pond on the right. Bright daylight, sky-blue top, warm cream and peach highlights, clean soft shapes, readable silhouette, pleasant uncluttered composition, gentle polished 2D mobile game art. Friendly for children. Landscape details concentrated near edges; leave the top quarter mostly clear sky and lower quarter simple grass/path so real UI can be placed over it. Portrait 2:3 composition. No text, no logo, no buttons, no HUD, no frames, no jewels or ornate decorative borders, no screenshots, no imitation of any existing game or brand.

The new navigation icons, hexagonal skill map, controls and panel shapes are native SVG/HTML/CSS. The old blue decorative frames and village backdrop are no longer shipped or referenced. Earlier AI-generated slime atlases, power icons, realm artwork and forge rune atlas are retained. Audio remains synthesized locally. No generation service, external font or CDN is required while playing.
