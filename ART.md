# Slimefall generated art

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. Game assets are included locally and do not require runtime image generation.

enemies.webp — a transparent 3×3 atlas. Prompt requested premium hand-painted pixel enemies: leaf slime, lightning runner, ice-armoured slime, mushroom poison slime, scarlet berserker, golden treasure slime, winged cyan companion, emerald crowned king and violet void monarch. Components were isolated and aligned into equal cells for reliable game sampling.
icons.webp — a transparent 4×4 atlas. Prompt requested magical sword, lightning, fireball, poison mushroom, snowflake, critical crosshair, twin arrows, spirit orb, heart, coins, crystal cluster, potion, meteor, vortex, clock and treasure chest. Components were isolated into equal cells.
frame.webp — a single empty obsidian RPG card frame with gold flourishes, emerald/amethyst corners and dark blue leather center. Used as a nine-slice interface frame with functional HTML text.
forest.webp — a portrait bioluminescent forest clearing, turquoise streams, mushrooms, ruins and purple crystals, with an uncluttered central combat space and a luminous bottom ward.
icon192.png and icon512.png — app icons derived from the generated companion sprite.

Sound effects and optional ambient chords are synthesized by the game with Web Audio. They are generated in code rather than stored audio recordings.

# Riftbound expansion art
Generated using the built-in image-generation tool, then aligned into isolated cells for runtime sampling.
enemies2.webp — transparent 4×3 atlas prompted with healer, mirror, splitter, teleporter, burrower, shield sentinel, storm caller, vampire leech, mushroom queen, lava knight, cosmic sovereign and treasure mimic. Distinct silhouettes, glossy hand-painted pixel style.
icons2.webp — transparent 4×3 atlas prompted with moon blades, laser prism, thunder cloud, returning glaive, frost mines, singularity, phoenix feather, thorn crown, curse skull, infinity blade, time hourglass and resonant heart.
effects.webp — transparent 2×2 effect atlas prompted with a face-on black hole and luminous accretion disk, cyan rune circle, gold explosive sunburst and green poison-cloud ring. Combined with procedural particles, lines and transforms.
biomes.webp — three equal portrait battlefields in one horizontal atlas. Prompt requested mushroom bog, volcanic forge and astral ruins with uncluttered dark central combat space.

All images are local files beside the scripts. No art or sound service is called during play.

# Soul Rush expansion art
Created with the built-in image-generation tool and incorporated as local files.
enemies3.webp — aligned 4×2 atlas. Prompt requested Bombcore, candy piñata slime, scarlet ninja duelist, pearl-shell slime, lantern ghost, hourglass jelly, storm-duelist boss and clockwork dragon-slime boss. A second image-generation edit removed the pale background while preserving those characters.
icons3.webp — aligned 4×2 atlas. Prompt requested sweeping sword, ruby weak-point bullseye, lightning-catching parry gauntlet, gem magnet, energy fist, bounty scroll, chromatic swords and chained hourglass.
Both sheets use the established glossy, detailed pixel-painted fantasy style. Functional effects use the existing generated effect atlas plus procedural sword trails, cue rings, particles and collection animations.
Sound is synthesized locally with Web Audio, including shaped noise for the slash and Soul Burst.

# Moonhaven art
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and included locally.
haven.webp — portrait storybook pixel village with a portal, rune forge on the left, moonlit fishing pond on the right, stargazer observatory, fountain and garden. Open upper sky and lower path accommodate functional UI.
moonframe.webp — silver-and-gold moonstone frame with turquoise corner jewels, a crescent crest and an empty sapphire velvet center. Used as a stretchable nine-slice frame for menus, cards, buttons and village labels.
havenicons.webp — aligned 4×3 atlas prompted with rune forge, fishing rod/moonfish, telescope, cottage, moon lantern, star fountain, lunar tree, stardust pouch and four distinct elemental rune stones.
The spatial puzzle uses exact code-drawn conduit connections. The generated art supplies its surrounding magical interface.
All art files are beside the scripts. No external image or font service is needed during play.
