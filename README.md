# Slimefall · Wild Ascension

A new portrait slime-combat roguelite. Its saves are independent of Tideforge.

## Mobile upload
Extract this ZIP. Upload ALL extracted files together at the root of your GitHub Pages repository. There are no asset folders and no build step.
If replacing Tideforge, replace index.html, app.js, style.css, manifest.json, sw.js and the two app icons. Upload engine.js and all four WebP files as well.
Open the published website online once to cache the game. Close and reopen it after deployment if the previous game still appears.
In Android Chrome, use the browser menu → Install app / Add to home screen.

## How to play
Begin a run. Tap or HOLD on slimes to strike. Drag your held finger across the crowd to change targets. Your spirit companion automatically attacks the lowest enemies.
Stop slimes before they cross the bottom ward. A normal escape costs one heart. A boss costs three.
Clear the wave and pick one of three cards. Effects stack for that run. One free reroll is available per wave.
Early waves guarantee a rare option. Boss-wave drafts guarantee an epic or legendary option while those cards remain available.
Every fifth wave includes a boss. Every tenth boss is the void monarch. Later waves spawn faster, with more enemies and more health.
Use Frost nova for three seconds of heavy slow. Overcharge doubles your damage for six seconds. Both recharge automatically.

## Build combinations
There are eighteen upgrade cards and sixteen illustrated skill icons.
Living lightning spreads Toxic bloom. Prism reactor strengthens lightning.
Permafrost lets Wildfire shatter slowed enemies for double splash damage.
Toxic bloom level 3 causes poison death-bursts. Plague monarch makes those explosions stronger.
Living lightning level 3 always chains. Wildfire level 3 increases its radius.
Twin spirits adds companion targets. Time thief accelerates volleys. Companion attacks inherit your card effects.
Falling stars adds automatic meteors. Event horizon periodically drags back and damages the whole swarm.
Perfect fracture enables critical builds. Royal decree executes weakened enemies. Soul feast and Second wind support longer runs.
Stacking damage, critical hits, splash, poison and automatic attacks can create very strong builds.

## Permanent progress and idle play
Coins and boss crystals are credited immediately and survive defeat.
Home → Upgrade offers permanent blade damage, starting hearts, companion bonuses, idle growth and improved card odds.
The sanctuary generates coins while playing or away. Away growth is capped at eight hours per visit and the bank holds up to 20,000 coins. Tap Collect to move coins into your wallet.
The companion fights automatically during an active battle. Combat pauses when you leave the game. Card choices need your input.
Home → Milestones offers one-time rewards. Grimoire tracks chosen cards.
Save & home or Pause lets you stop a run and resume it later, including an unfinished card choice.
Automatic saves occur regularly and when the page is hidden. Settings contains export/import and a confirmed reset.
Slimefall has its own save key, so replacing the website does not erase stored Tideforge progress. Tideforge saves cannot be imported into Slimefall.

## Sound and comfort
Procedural effects cover attacks, pops, critical hits, coins, powers, cards, purchases, damage and menus.
Settings offers sound, ambient music, haptics and screen shake toggles. Music starts off.
Audio needs a user gesture and support from the browser. Haptics depend on the device.
Reduced-motion system preferences disable decorative CSS animation.
